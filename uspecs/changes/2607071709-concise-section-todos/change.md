# Change request: Concise section todo guidance

## Why

Plan sections can become hard to scan when todo items restate full specifications or implementation detail. Shared guidance should keep section todos compact while still preserving enough direction for agents to create, update, or review the referenced artifacts.

## What

Readers gain consistent section-item guidance for uspecs planning skills:

- New artifact todos use short descriptions that name purpose and the source material to follow
- Small update todos stay precise about the target and intended change
- Large update todos are compacted and delegate detailed acceptance criteria, rationale, edge cases, or workflow branches to referenced artifacts
- The shared todo guidance fits Domain design, Functional design, Provisioning and configuration, Technical design, and Construction section skills
